The Quiet Shore

A fifteen-year-old is found dead at the foot of a cliff path on a remote Norwegian island. The case is closed as an accident. Eight weeks later, her mother sends a notebook. Detective Ingrid Nygaard arrives on Ryvær to find a community that has been managing what it knows for eleven years — and a girl who was, quietly, right.
