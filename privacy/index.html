We may use third-party services for analytics, email delivery, and hosting. These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Our site may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and to gather analytics data. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
